Our Story

About BLACKLINE

BLACKLINE BARBERS started with a simple idea: a haircut should feel like a ritual, not an errand.

We brought that idea to Doha in 2018. Since then, we've built a team of barbers who treat every cut as a craft — not a transaction. No two heads are the same, so we take the time to understand what you want before we pick up the clippers.

Our space is calm, our tools are sharp, and our standards are high. Whether it's your first visit or your fiftieth, you'll leave looking sharp and feeling like yourself.
